PROJECT 03 / POWER ELECTRONICS

Drone Power Board

A power distribution board with independent primary and backup supply paths for a drone.

4-LAYER PCBREDUNDANT POWERSOFT-STARTSTM32

01 / OVERVIEW

Power with a backup plan

This four-layer PCB was designed to manage a primary power path and an independent redundant backup supply. A controlled soft-start mechanism pre-charges the high-capacitance loads in the motor controllers to limit inrush current on startup.

Key design elements

  • Two independent power supply paths: primary and backup
  • Soft-start by pre-charging motor controller loads
  • Inrush current limitation during startup
  • STM32-family microcontroller for voltage monitoring and power management
  • Communication with the drone control system
